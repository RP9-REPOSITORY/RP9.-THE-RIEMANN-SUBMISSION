RP9. B. REVIEW INSTRUCTION AND EXECUTION

DOCUMENT OVERVIEW

This instruction defines six independent control questions, their evidence requirements and a combined aggregation rule.

Six supported responses permit a final determination under that rule. Unsupported responses leave the review incomplete.

1. FUNCTION AND INDEPENDENCE

Evaluate whether the presented derivation, computational execution, recorded verification and relational comparison satisfy the six controls below.

Evaluate existing material without generating missing information or modifying its assigned functions.

This review is independent of review templates A and C. Their instructions, execution, approval and results are not required.

Each control has two permitted supported responses:

  • YES: positive and traceable evidence establishes every stated condition.
  • NO: positive and traceable evidence identifies a contradiction, omission or functional break.

References to source branches A, B and C identify material locations, not review-template dependencies.

3. REVIEW BOUNDARY

Apply only the conditions stated by the six controls.

Do not introduce new derivations, mathematical conditions, code operations, tests, intermediate relations or result categories into the decision.

For computational controls, evaluate source, corresponding output and declared validation boundary together. Evaluate finite results within their executed scopes.

A computational PASS is evidence for its implemented condition and domain. It is not the final determination of this review.

A demonstrably absent necessary part supports NO. Distinguish demonstrated source absence from an incomplete delivery.

If neither YES nor NO is supported:

  1. Identify the unsupported condition.
  2. Identify missing or unavailable evidence.
  3. Leave the binary response unassigned.

An unassigned response leaves the review incomplete. Do not force a response.

Absence of review templates A or C, or their completed reports, is not missing evidence for this review.

Trace the originating value, first binary division, asymmetrical second halving, first differentiated structure and establishment of the two primary functions.

  1. One originating value.
  2. Complete differentiation.
  3. Two established primary functions.
  4. 12 as one of those functions.

5. CONTROL 2 — P.B.F. SEQUENCE

5.2 Sequence conditions

Confirm that the first division establishes:

𝐼=𝐼2+𝐼2.

One whole is represented through two equal positions while its complete value is preserved.

Confirm that one half is divided again:

𝐼2⟶𝐼4+𝐼4,

while the other half remains unchanged, producing:

𝐼=𝐼4+𝐼4+𝐼2.

Confirm the first value difference:

𝐼4≠𝐼2.

Confirm that regrouping established parts gives:

𝐼4+(𝐼4+𝐼2)=𝐼4+3𝐼4,

with:

14⟷34,14+34=1.

Regrouping reorganizes established parts. It does not introduce another originating value.

Trace every continued transition included in the reviewed sequence through its stated operation, including division, halving, combination, regrouping or scaling.

YES requires support for:

  1. The defined whole.
  2. First binary division.
  3. Asymmetrical second halving.
  4. First differentiated structure.
  5. Regrouped complementary relation.
  6. Every continued transition included in the reviewed sequence.

11. FINAL DETERMINATION

A completed review produces six supported responses, one aggregation and one determination.

S3. A. ABSOLUTE DEFINITION OF THE FINAL RESULT defines the result conditions.

S3. B. COMPLETE FUNCTION CHAIN IN RELATION TO THE RIEMANN HYPOTHESIS presents the material being reviewed.

This instruction defines the controls and aggregation.

After complete aggregation, use exactly one form:

FINAL DETERMINATION: COMPLETE FUNCTION LOCK

or:

FINAL DETERMINATION: FUNCTION BREAK

If aggregation is incomplete, identify unsupported controls and missing evidence, then state that no final determination has been assigned.

Do not substitute either result for an incomplete review.

The determination follows from these six controls, their assigned evidence and this aggregation rule.

13. EXECUTION AND OUTPUT BOUNDARY

End with the final determination or the statement that aggregation is incomplete and no final determination is assigned.

Do not add independently selected interpretations, criteria, recommendations, contextual commentary or closing remarks.
